For local businesses

We Help Local Businesses Get Seen

At The List Ann Arbor, we feature and promote locally owned businesses to help spread awareness throughout the community. Our growing audience of followers and email subscribers actively supports local businesses and loves discovering new places around Ann Arbor.

When we feature a business, we come out to create content, highlight your story, and run a giveaway connected to your business valued at $50 or more. That could be a gift card, a service, a product, or an experience.

If you're interested in being featured, fill out the form below and our team will reach out if your business is a good fit.
