Our story

About The List Ann Arbor

The List Ann Arbor is a free weekly giveaway that celebrates the locally owned businesses that give our city its character. Each week we feature a local business, share its story, and give one subscriber the chance to win something from it.

Meet the team

Founder

Dr. Nic Mueller is an Ann Arbor chiropractor, entrepreneur, and community advocate passionate about helping people and supporting local businesses. As the owner of The Bridge Chiropractic, he is dedicated to serving the Ann Arbor community and helping others live healthier, more connected lives.
